Repo pipeline: RR-feature ECG analyzer

A JavaScript port of the logic in ECG-Anomaly-Detection-Using-Deep-Learning (RRfeatures.m and RealTimeGUI.m): baseline removal, smoothing, R-peak detection at 0.45 x max, RR intervals, heart rate, HRV, NN50, then the GUI's heart-rate condition and advice rules. The repo ships no data, so signals are synthetic at 300 Hz (the PhysioNet 2017 rate); to analyze your own recording, use the upload section below. The rhythm label comes from an RR-irregularity rule, not the repo's trained GRU/LSTM ensemble.

Evolution lab

A genetic algorithm searches four detector settings (baseline window, smoothing, threshold, integration window) on three noisy training records. Fitness is F1 score minus a penalty for R-peak timing jitter. Elitism keeps the best solution each generation.

How it works

  1. Clean

    Moving-average baseline removal keeps QRS amplitude close to true while removing wander.

  2. Locate

    Slope-energy detector with an adaptive threshold and a 200 ms refractory period finds R-peaks.

  3. Judge

    Fuzzy rules weigh rate, regularity and signal quality into an alarm confidence.

  4. Alert

    Confidence of 0.6 or more raises the alarm. Noise-like input is held for review instead.

Hard constraints from the brief

Speed

Ventricular fibrillation and tachycardia alert within 3 seconds.

Safety

False-alarm suppression must not silence acute events.

Fidelity

QRS amplitude and ST elevation are preserved by the filters.

Footprint

A fixed 6 KB streaming buffer suits wearable hardware.
